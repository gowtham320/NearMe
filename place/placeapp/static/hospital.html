Aakash Hospital is a 120 bed hospital that is well-known for its patient-centric approach, advanced healthcare infrastructure and competent medical care. The hospital has experienced, qualified and highly trained doctors who diagnose, treat and surgically manage the patients who visit Aakash Hospital. The hospital caters to several medical specialties, including Cardiology, General Surgery, Gynecology and Obstetrics, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Pulmonology, Gastroenterology, Oncology, Dermatology, Plastic Surgery. All ailments pertaining to these specialties are taken care of by the expert doctors at Aakash Hospital. It provides the following services to their patients for a comprehensive and efficient healthcare experience: Path Lab, CT Scan, X Ray, Pharmacy.
