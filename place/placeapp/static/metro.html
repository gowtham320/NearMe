Thiruvottiyur Metro Station is an elevated station on the Blue Line of the Chennai Metro, inaugurated on February 14, 2021, serving the Thiruvottiyur area and its surroundings. Key Details Location: Thiruvottiyur Metro Station is situated in the northern part of Chennai, India. It serves the neighborhoods of Thiruvottiyur and connects to other northern suburbs, enhancing urban mobility in the region. Inauguration: The station was officially opened on February 14, 2021, as part of the northern extension of the Blue Line of Phase I of the Chennai Metro.
